Questions

Which crystal is used for which chakra?

In common crystal traditions, red stones such as ruby are linked to the root chakra, yellow stones such as citrine to the solar plexus, green stones such as emerald to the heart, blue stones such as sapphire to the throat, and violet stones such as amethyst to the crown.

Which frequency goes with which chakra?

A widely used pairing is 396 Hz for the root, 528 Hz for the solar plexus, 639 Hz for the heart, 741 Hz for the throat and 963 Hz for the crown. These pairings come from meditation practice, not from scientific research.

What are solfeggio frequencies?

A set of tones (174, 285, 396, 417, 528, 639, 741, 852 and 963 Hz) popular in meditation music. People use them as a calm focus for relaxation; claims that they heal the body are not supported by evidence.
