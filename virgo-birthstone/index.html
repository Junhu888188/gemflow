What Is the Virgo Birthstone?

By Jun Hu

You're the girl who helps everyone write the perfect message to their crush, then spends half an hour editing your own "hey." People think you have everything figured out. That's a rather generous assumption. Virgo (August 23 to September 22) has a birthstone called carnelian, whose warm orange-red reveals your surprisingly romantic inner world.

Dates
August 23 to September 22
Birthstone
Carnelian
Color
Warm orange-red
In one line
Has everything organized, secretly a hopeless romantic.

The Virgo everyone knows

Why Does Everyone Think Virgo Has Everything Together?

You're the friend who checks the weather before going out, remembers where everyone parked, and spots the spelling mistake five seconds after someone posts a picture.

You've probably made a list of things you need to make lists for.

When your best friend has a terrible day, you're already looking up places to take her for dinner.

You show affection in wonderfully practical little ways, sometimes without realizing you're doing it.

Life feels slightly less complicated when someone has a friend like you.

The Virgo nobody sees

Are You Secretly a Hopeless Romantic?

Beneath that sensible exterior lives a girl who imagines being kissed in the rain.

You secretly love handwritten letters. You remember compliments for months, although your immediate response is usually to change the subject.

You've imagined someone turning up at your door simply because they missed you.

In George Kunz's 1913 book The Curious Lore of Precious Stones, Virgo's stone is carnelian.

Its warm orange-red mirrors the romantic warmth you keep tucked away behind all that careful thinking.

Being sensible has never stopped you from wanting something magical to happen to you.

Behind the perfectly organized exterior

Why Does Virgo Overthink Everything?

Someone tells you to stop overthinking, as though you've simply forgotten where the off button is.

Very helpful.

You notice when someone's tone changes halfway through a conversation. You wonder whether you've said something wrong, then mentally review everything you said that afternoon.

When you suggest a better way of doing something, you're often just trying to make someone's life easier.

Unfortunately, your affection occasionally sounds like a list of instructions.

People notice how much attention you pay to details. They rarely realize how much those details mean to you.

When everything can wait until tomorrow

What If Nothing Needed to Be Perfect?

It's 2 a.m. Your desk is tidy, your plans for tomorrow are written down, and you're watching the same romantic scene for the fourth time.

You open your journal and write something sweet about your crush, then close it before you can criticize your own handwriting.

You imagine an afternoon with absolutely nothing planned. Someone holds your hand, and somehow you forget to check the time.

Maybe warm orange is the color of everything you wish you could enjoy without analyzing.

For once, nothing needs improving. Especially you.

When your warm orange side wants out, Gemflow turns citrine into slow-moving golden light on your phone, so you can watch that glow for once.

Carnelian is the Virgo birthstone, a warm orange-red that mirrors the unexpected romantic hiding inside you.
