396 Hz: the root chakra frequency, and what we actually know

396 Hz is a warm, low tone that vibrates 396 times a second, just above the G over middle C. It is the first of the Solfeggio frequencies, paired with the root chakra and labelled “liberating guilt and fear”. The label comes from a 1999 book, and no study has tested the tone itself. Below: the history, the meaning, safety, and how to listen.

The basics

What is 396 Hz?

396 Hz means 396 vibrations per second. In standard tuning (A above middle C = 440 Hz) the nearest note is the G above middle C, at 392 Hz; 396 Hz sits about 18 cents (a sixth of a semitone) above it. It is the lowest of the six original Solfeggio frequencies, and most lists pair it with the root chakra at the base of the spine.

The history

Where did 396 Hz come from?

It is the first number in the list made famous by Leonard Horowitz’s book Healing Codes for the Biological Apocalypse (1999). According to Horowitz, the herbalist Joseph Puleo found the six numbers in the verse numbers of the Book of Numbers, chapter 7, from verse 12. Horowitz matched them to the singing syllables of the medieval hymn Ut queant laxis, which Guido of Arezzo used to teach singing around 1025. 396 Hz became “Ut”, the syllable that later turned into “do”.

The catch

Is it really an ancient tone?

The hymn is old, but Guido’s syllables named the steps of a scale, not fixed pitches: pitch was not measured in hertz until the 19th century. The number 396 comes from the 1990s, not from medieval chant.

The label

What does 396 Hz mean?

Horowitz’s list gives 396 Hz the label “liberating guilt and fear”, and today it is usually paired with the root chakra, the one tied to safety and feeling grounded. Both are traditions, not research findings. If the idea helps you, it can be a simple intention to sit with: something to put down for ten minutes.

The research

Does 396 Hz do anything?

We found no peer-reviewed study that tested 396 Hz on its own. The only Solfeggio tone with even a small human study is 528 Hz: nine adults had lower cortisol after five minutes of piano music tuned to it. Calm music and slow breathing help many people relax, and a low, steady 396 Hz tone can be part of that. There is no evidence that it removes guilt, fear or “blockages”.

Source: Akimoto K et al., Effect of 528 Hz Music on the Endocrine System and Autonomic Nervous System, Health 10(9), 2018

Safety

Is 396 Hz safe?

Yes, at a comfortable volume. It is an ordinary audible tone, close to a note you would hear in any song. The only real risk is loudness: the WHO and ITU safe-listening standard suggests keeping personal audio around 80 decibels or lower, for no more than 40 hours a week.

Source: WHO–ITU, Global standard for safe listening devices and systems (H.870, 2019)

How to listen

What does 396 Hz sound like?

A warm, steady hum, close to the G above middle C. It is the lowest of the five tones in Gemflow and the easiest to listen to for a long time. Most people play it softly under music or nature sounds. Sit or lie down, let your weight sink, and breathe slowly for ten minutes.
