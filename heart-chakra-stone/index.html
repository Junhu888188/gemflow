Guide · the heart chakra

Which stone really belongs to the heart chakra?

Every list gives a different answer: rose quartz, emerald, green aventurine, jade, malachite. So we went back to the oldest text we could find. Short answer: in 1577 the heart lotus was red, in 1927 it was gold, and green and pink are modern. Each belongs to its own tradition.

Today

Why does every list give a different answer?

Open five crystal guides and you will find five heart-chakra stones. They fall into two camps: green (emerald, green aventurine, jade, malachite) and pink (rose quartz). Neither camp is wrong — they come from different eras of the chakra story.

1577 · red

What colour is the heart lotus in the old text?

“Of the shining colour of the Bandhuka flower.”

That is how the Ṣaṭ-Cakra-Nirūpaṇa (verse 22) describes the heart lotus. The Bandhuka is a red flower (Pentapetes phoenicea). The lotus has twelve petals, and its name, Anāhata, means the sound that arises without two things striking. The text was written by Pūrṇānanda in 1577 as the sixth chapter of his Śrī-tattva-cintāmaṇi. It names no gemstone at all.

Source: Arthur Avalon (Sir John Woodroffe), The Serpent Power (1919), English translation of the Ṣaṭ-Cakra-Nirūpaṇa

1927 · gold

Was the heart chakra ever gold?

“The fourth centre, at the heart, is of a glowing golden colour.”

So wrote C. W. Leadbeater in The Chakras (1927), describing it as a wheel with twelve spokes, the same twelve as the old lotus petals.

Source: C. W. Leadbeater, The Chakras (1927)

20th century · green

So where did green come from?

Later in the 20th century, Western writers matched the seven chakras to the seven colours of the rainbow. Counting up from red at the root, the fourth chakra lands on green, and green stones followed. Who first made the rainbow match is disputed, so we do not name anyone here. For the full history, see Kurt Leland’s Rainbow Body (2016).

Modern · pink

And where does pink fit in?

Pink is the colour of love in modern crystal books, and that is how rose quartz came to the heart chakra. We did not find pink in any of the older texts we read.

Verdict

So which one is right?

1577 says red. 1927 says gold. Today says green, or pink. Each answer belongs to its own tradition. If a stone helps you sit still and breathe, it is the right one for you.

Questions

What is the stone for the heart chakra?

Modern guides most often name green stones (emerald, green aventurine, jade, malachite) or pink rose quartz. The oldest Sanskrit text on the chakras, from 1577, describes the heart lotus as red and names no stone.

What colour is the heart chakra?

Green in the modern rainbow system. The 1577 Ṣaṭ-Cakra-Nirūpaṇa describes it as red like the Bandhuka flower; Leadbeater (1927) described it as glowing gold.

What does Anahata mean?

Anāhata is Sanskrit for “unstruck”: the sound that arises without two things striking each other.

Do heart chakra stones heal?

There is no scientific evidence that stones heal. People use them for meditation, ritual and as a reminder of an intention.
