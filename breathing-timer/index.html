How to use the breathing timer

Choose a crystal, a breathing pattern and a session of 1, 3 or 5 minutes. Press Start breathing and follow the words and countdown as the light grows with each inhale and settles with each exhale.

Pause whenever you like, then press Resume breathing to continue. Press End to finish at any time. Switching away from this page pauses the timer; resume when you are ready. The tool is free, with no account, ads or sound.

Choose a breathing pattern

Gentle · 4 in / 6 out

Breathe in for four seconds and out for six, without holding your breath. This is the default; use Custom if you prefer shorter counts.

Box · 4-4-4-4

Follow four equal parts: breathe in, hold, breathe out, hold, each for four seconds. Holds are optional in your practice: switch to Gentle or set them to zero in Custom if they feel uncomfortable.

4-7-8

Breathe in for four seconds, hold for seven and breathe out for eight. This pattern includes a longer hold; choose it only if it feels natural, and stop or return to normal breathing whenever needed.

Custom

Set each part in whole seconds. Choose 2–10 seconds for each inhale and exhale, and 0–10 seconds for each hold; a zero-second hold is skipped.

Breathing safely

This timer is designed for general relaxation and mindfulness, not medical treatment.

Sit or lie down somewhere comfortable. Breathe gently and never force your breathing. If a pattern feels uncomfortable, shorten the counts, skip breath holds, or return to your normal breathing.

Stop if you feel dizzy, faint, short of breath, or uncomfortable.

If you have a heart or respiratory condition, or are unsure whether breath-holding exercises are suitable for you, speak with a qualified healthcare professional before trying them.

Do not use this timer while driving, swimming, or operating machinery.
