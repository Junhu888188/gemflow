How Gemflow differs from Headspace

Headspace covers a lot: guided meditation, structured courses, sleep content, movement, mental health coaching and an AI companion called Ebb, with more than 1,000 expert-led exercises.3

Gemflow is a Headspace alternative built around a single experience. You choose a gemstone, choose a length, and breathe with its light. With My rhythm, the light learns your own pace. Nothing needs a subscription, and the free gemstone has every feature the others have.

How Gemflow differs from Calm

Calm offers guided meditation, Sleep Stories, music, breathing exercises and background Scenes, with some free content and a lifetime membership option.67

As a Calm alternative, Gemflow puts everything into what you see and hear while you meditate. The gemstone light is drawn live on your phone, not played as a looped video. The sound is guqin we recorded ourselves, real singing bowls and ocean waves, with each stone's frequency tone mixed in so softly you can barely hear it. One button turns all sound off.

How Gemflow differs from Insight Timer

Insight Timer has a very large free library, a meditation timer, groups, live events and courses, with an optional MemberPlus subscription.1620

Gemflow is an Insight Timer alternative for meditating on your own. There are no groups, feeds or accounts. You choose your gemstone and your time, then begin. Your meditation stays on your phone, and the app works offline.

A meditation app without a subscription

Gemflow is free to download. The first time you open it, one gemstone chooses you, and it is yours to keep, free. Every feature works with it: My rhythm, all five lengths, the sound controls and offline use.

If you feel closer to another stone's light, you can buy that one gemstone, or all five together. Each is a one-time purchase. There is no monthly fee and there are no ads.

How to meditate with Gemflow

  1. Choose your gemstone. Start with the one that chose you, or another you own.
  2. Choose your time. 1, 3, 5, 10 or 20 minutes.
  3. Follow the light. Hold to breathe in, let go to breathe out. After a few breaths the light learns your pace; next time, choose My rhythm.
  4. Choose your sound. Guqin, singing bowls and waves, or turn everything off and sit in silence.

Questions

Is Gemflow free?

Yes. Gemflow is free to download and your first gemstone is free to keep. Every feature works without paying. Extra gemstones are optional one-time purchases.

Do I need an account to use Gemflow?

No. You open the app and begin. There is no sign-up, no analytics, and your meditation stays on your phone.

Does Gemflow have a subscription?

No. There is no monthly or yearly fee. The only purchases are extra gemstones, one at a time or all five together.

Can I use Gemflow offline?

Yes. Gemflow works without an internet connection, and you do not need a subscription to meditate offline.

Does Gemflow have a community?

No. Gemflow is made for meditating on your own. There are no groups, followers, messages or feeds.

Is Gemflow a medical product?

No. Gemflow is a meditation app, not a medical product. Its light, breathing and sound are made for meditation.
