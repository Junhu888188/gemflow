When your light feels dim

Have you been living by everyone’s wishes but your own?

Perhaps you are the one who keeps the peace, who notices what everyone else needs before they ask. Somewhere along the way your own voice grew faint. You long to wake up, to feel that your life is moving towards something, but you are not sure what you want, or whether you are allowed to want it. That longing for direction is not a weakness. In the old picture of the body, the sense of direction itself lives here, in the warm center of you.

Manipura · Maṇipūra

A city of jewels inside you

The Sanskrit name of the solar plexus chakra is Manipura (Maṇipūra). It holds the word maṇi, jewel, and is often translated “city of jewels”. An old Tantric commentary gives its own reason for the name: because of the fire that lives in it, the lotus is “lustrous as a gem”. What a beautiful thing to know about yourself: that at your center there is something that shines. In yoga traditions this chakra is linked with will, self-worth, and the power to turn a wish into a step. Many people feel it as a warmth, or a tightness, just below the ribs when they are about to speak up.

Rain clouds and fire

The lotus that hides the rising sun

“Above it, and at the root of the navel, is the shining Lotus of ten petals, of the colour of heavy-laden rain-clouds.”

That is how the Sat-Chakra-Nirupana (Ṣaṭ-Cakra-Nirūpaṇa), written by Pūrṇānanda in Bengal in 1577, pictures the solar plexus lotus: ten petals dark as storm clouds, and at its heart a triangle of fire “shining like the rising sun”. On the petals are letters the color of the blue lotus. The fire’s seed sound is Raṃ, and its god rides a ram. Rain clouds wrapped around a sunrise: it is the most hopeful picture in the whole old map. Your fire was never put out. Sometimes it is simply behind the clouds.

Citrine

A golden light to find your own shine

Today the solar plexus is painted yellow, the color of sunlight and late gold, and citrine is its classic stone. Citrine is traditionally seen as a stone of warmth, optimism and confidence; the pairing follows the shared color, not an old text. Many people keep a piece of citrine close on days that ask for courage: in a pocket before a difficult conversation, on a desk while they start something new. It does not give you a fire you do not have. It reminds you of the one you do.

Your inner sun

From the earth to the fire

In the old picture the chakras rise like lotuses along the spine. Below the solar plexus lies the root chakra, red and steady, the earth that holds you. Above it lies the heart. The fire sits between them: once you feel held, your own warmth can begin to rise. And there is an older thread in the name “solar” plexus. The small sun at your center answers the great sun above you. When you sit with your inner fire, you are not only turning inward; you are remembering that you are made of the same light as the sky.

The rainbow road

How the fire changed its colors

Of all the chakras, the solar plexus has worn the most different colors on its way to us: storm-dark, then red and green, then gold.

  1. 1577 · In Bengal, Pūrṇānanda writes the Sat-Chakra-Nirupana (Ṣaṭ-Cakra-Nirūpaṇa). The navel lotus has ten petals dark as rain clouds, and a fire inside like the rising sun.
  2. 1919 · Sir John Woodroffe, writing as Arthur Avalon, carries the text into English as The Serpent Power.
  3. 1927 · C. W. Leadbeater, in The Chakras, describes the navel center as a wheel of ten spokes: “a curious blending of several shades of red, though there is also a great deal of green in it.”
  4. Today · Over the 20th century the seven chakras came to be painted as a rainbow, and the solar plexus took the gold between orange and green. Every age has seen the same fire in its own color.

Questions

What color is the solar plexus chakra?

The solar plexus chakra is yellow in the modern system. In the 1577 Sat-Chakra-Nirupana its ten petals are dark as rain clouds around a red triangle of fire; Leadbeater (1927) described it as red and green.

What does Manipura mean?

Manipura (Maṇipūra) is the Sanskrit name of the solar plexus chakra, often translated “city of jewels”. Avalon’s Serpent Power (1919) explains it as a lotus “lustrous as a gem” because of its inner fire.

Where do the solar plexus chakra’s colors come from?

The solar plexus chakra’s colors come from three layers: the 1577 Sat-Chakra-Nirupana (rain-cloud petals, fiery triangle), Leadbeater’s The Chakras (1927, red and green), and the 20th-century rainbow system (yellow).

Which stone goes with the solar plexus chakra?

Citrine is the stone most often paired with the solar plexus chakra, following its modern yellow color; tiger’s eye and yellow jasper are also common. The older Sanskrit texts do not name a stone.

Which frequency goes with the solar plexus chakra?

The solar plexus chakra is usually paired with 528 Hz, a Solfeggio frequency, in modern meditation music. The pairing is modern practice, not from the older texts. In Gemflow, citrine carries 528 Hz.

How do I meditate with citrine?

To meditate with citrine, sit tall, hold the stone just above your navel and breathe slowly for a few minutes, imagining a golden flame growing brighter there. End with one sentence of intention.

Can citrine heal me?

Citrine is not a medical treatment. People use it as a companion for meditation and ritual, and as a reminder of their own confidence and warmth.
