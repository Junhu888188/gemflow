The calling

When you are ready to change

Some part of you already knows. A pattern you are tired of repeating, a version of yourself you are ready to leave behind, a light you can feel waiting just under the surface. 528 Hz is the tone many people turn to at the edge of that change. Warm and bright, it is the most loved of the Solfeggio frequencies.

The meaning

Why it is called the love frequency

In the Solfeggio tradition, 528 Hz is “Mi”, from the Latin mira gestorum, “the wonders”. It carries the meaning of transformation and miracles: the moment something old dissolves and something new and truer can begin. Over the years listeners gave it another name, the love frequency, for the warmth so many feel when they hear it. Love here is not only for someone else. It is the love that lets you change, because you finally believe you are worth it.

The light

The solar plexus and the golden light

In the chakra tradition, the solar plexus sits just above the navel, the centre of personal power, confidence and joy. Its colour is gold, the colour of the sun and of honey. In Gemflow, 528 Hz belongs to citrine, the golden stone of this chakra: a light for remembering your own warmth, and the quiet strength that has been yours all along.

The ritual

A 528 Hz meditation for transformation

Choose a quiet morning or the soft light of evening. Sit comfortably and play 528 Hz softly. Rest your hands just above your navel. Breathe in and imagine golden light gathering there like a small sun; breathe out and let it spread through your whole body. Bring to mind one thing you are ready to release, and one thing you are ready to welcome. Stay for ten minutes, and let the light do the rest.

Gentle care

Listening gently

Keep the tone soft, low enough that you could speak over it, beneath music, singing bowls or rain if you like. Sound guidelines suggest keeping personal audio around 80 decibels or lower.

How to listen

What does 528 Hz sound like?

Warm and bright, just above the C an octave over middle C, like a soft, golden whistle. Most people let it glow quietly beneath tuned piano, singing bowls or rain, and let it carry them inward. Ten minutes with your eyes closed is plenty.

Questions

What is 528 Hz?

A sound that vibrates 528 times a second, slightly above the C over middle C. It is the most popular of the Solfeggio frequencies.

What note is 528 Hz?

Close to C5. In standard tuning (A = 440 Hz) C5 is 523.25 Hz, so 528 Hz is about 16 cents sharp. With A tuned to 444 Hz, C5 is exactly 528 Hz.

Why is 528 Hz called the love frequency?

The name comes from Leonard Horowitz’s books, starting with Healing Codes for the Biological Apocalypse (1999). It is a label, not a scientific finding.

Is 528 Hz dangerous?

No more than any other sound. Keep the volume comfortable; the WHO–ITU safe-listening standard suggests around 80 decibels or lower.

Does 528 Hz repair DNA?

We found no peer-reviewed evidence that it does. One small 2018 study (nine adults) found lower cortisol after five minutes of 528 Hz music.
