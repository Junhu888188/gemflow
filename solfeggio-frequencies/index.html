Solfeggio frequencies: all nine, explained

The Solfeggio frequencies are a set of sound tones, from 174 to 963 Hz, used for meditation and relaxation. There were six to begin with (396, 417, 528, 639, 741 and 852 Hz); later lists added 963 to make seven, one per chakra, and then 174 and 285 to make nine. Here is the full chart, their real history, and what is known about them.

The basics

What are Solfeggio frequencies?

They are specific sound frequencies, measured in hertz (Hz), that people listen to while meditating, resting or falling asleep: usually as a pure tone, or with music tuned to them. The name borrows the old word for sight-singing, solfeggio (do, re, mi), because the first six were linked to the syllables Ut, Re, Mi, Fa, Sol and La.

The history

Where did the Solfeggio frequencies come from?

The six original numbers were made famous by Leonard Horowitz’s book Healing Codes for the Biological Apocalypse (1999). According to Horowitz, the herbalist Joseph Puleo found them in the verse numbers of the Book of Numbers, chapter 7, and Horowitz tied them to the medieval hymn Ut queant laxis, whose syllables Guido of Arezzo used to teach singing around 1025. Guido’s syllables named the steps of a scale, not frequencies, so the numbers themselves are modern. The full story of the most famous one is on our 528 Hz page.

Six, seven, nine

Why do some lists have 7 and others 9?

The original set has six. Later lists added 963 Hz, giving seven tones that line up with the seven chakras: this is what people usually mean by “the 7 healing frequencies”. Other lists added 174 and 285 Hz below the six, giving nine. We could not find who first added them, so we do not name anyone.

The research

Do healing frequencies work?

Calm music helps many people relax, and that part is well studied. The specific numbers have very little research behind them. For 528 Hz there is one small study: nine adults had lower cortisol after five minutes of piano music tuned to 528 Hz than after the same music at standard pitch. We found no comparable studies for the other frequencies. The labels in the chart (“letting go of fear”, “miracles”) are traditions and promises from the books that made the tones popular, not research findings.

Source: Akimoto K et al., Effect of 528 Hz Music on the Endocrine System and Autonomic Nervous System, Health 10(9), 2018

Safety

Are Solfeggio frequencies safe?

Yes, at a comfortable volume. They are ordinary audible tones; the only real risk with any sound is loudness. The WHO and ITU safe-listening standard suggests keeping personal audio around 80 decibels or lower, for no more than 40 hours a week.

Source: WHO–ITU, Global standard for safe listening devices and systems (H.870, 2019)

How to listen

How do you use them?

Pick one tone, keep the volume low, close your eyes and breathe slowly for ten minutes. A pure tone can feel thin on its own, so most people prefer it under music or nature sounds. There is no need to listen to all nine: choose the one whose number or colour you are drawn to.

Questions

What are the 9 Solfeggio frequencies?

174, 285, 396, 417, 528, 639, 741, 852 and 963 Hz. The original six are 396, 417, 528, 639, 741 and 852 Hz; 963, 174 and 285 were added later.

What are the 7 healing frequencies?

Usually 396, 417, 528, 639, 741, 852 and 963 Hz: the six original Solfeggio tones plus 963 Hz, paired with the seven chakras from root to crown.

Which chakra goes with which frequency?

In the common modern pairing: 396 Hz root, 417 Hz sacral, 528 Hz solar plexus, 639 Hz heart, 741 Hz throat, 852 Hz third eye and 963 Hz crown.

Where do Solfeggio frequencies come from?

They were made famous by Leonard Horowitz’s 1999 book Healing Codes for the Biological Apocalypse, which credits Joseph Puleo with finding them in the Book of Numbers. They are not medieval, although they borrow the names of medieval singing syllables.

Do Solfeggio frequencies work?

Calm music helps many people relax. For the specific numbers there is very little research: one small 2018 study found lower cortisol after 528 Hz music. There is no evidence that they heal illness.

Is 432 Hz a Solfeggio frequency?

No. 432 Hz is a proposed tuning for the note A, instead of the standard 440 Hz. It is a separate idea that is often mixed up with the Solfeggio list.

Which Solfeggio frequency is the most popular?

528 Hz, often called the “love frequency”, is by far the most searched and the most listened to.
