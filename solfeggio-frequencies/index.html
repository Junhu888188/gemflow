Solfeggio frequencies: nine tones for the inner journey

The Solfeggio frequencies are nine sacred tones, from 174 to 963 Hz, that people around the world listen to in meditation. Each carries its own meaning, and seven of them rise like a ladder through the chakras, from the root to the crown. Here is the full chart, what each tone means, and how to find the one that is calling you.

The calling

What are the Solfeggio frequencies?

They are tones, each a single, pure frequency, that people return to when they want to slow down and listen inward. Some play them as a soft hum beneath music; some simply sit in silence with one note for ten minutes. The name comes from solfeggio, the old way of singing do, re, mi, because the first six tones were linked to the syllables Ut, Re, Mi, Fa, Sol and La of a medieval hymn.

The ladder

A ladder of light through the chakras

Seven of the tones are paired with the seven chakras, so listening to them in order is like climbing a ladder of light: 396 Hz to feel grounded and release fear at the root; 417 Hz for change at the sacral centre; 528 Hz, the love frequency, for transformation at the solar plexus; 639 Hz for love and connection at the heart; 741 Hz for expression and intuition at the throat; 852 Hz for inner sight at the third eye; and 963 Hz, the frequency of the gods, for connection to something greater at the crown.

Choosing

Which one is calling you?

There is no need to listen to all nine. Read the list slowly and notice which number, which colour or which meaning you feel drawn to; that is usually the one you need right now. If you feel scattered or afraid, start low, at the root. If your heart feels closed, begin at 639. If you are reaching for something beyond yourself, go to the crown. And if you keep hearing about 432 Hz: it is a gentle way of tuning music rather than a Solfeggio tone, 432 Hz or 528 Hz, which one is calling you.

The ritual

A simple Solfeggio meditation

Choose your tone and a quiet ten minutes. Play it softly, close your eyes and breathe slowly. Imagine the colour of its chakra glowing in that part of your body, a little brighter with each breath. When thoughts come, let the tone carry them away. Afterwards, sit for one more breath before you open your eyes.

Gentle care

Listening gently

Keep every tone soft, low enough that you could speak over it, beneath music, singing bowls or rain if you like. Sound guidelines suggest keeping personal audio around 80 decibels or lower.

Questions

What are the 9 Solfeggio frequencies?

174, 285, 396, 417, 528, 639, 741, 852 and 963 Hz. The original six are 396, 417, 528, 639, 741 and 852 Hz; 963, 174 and 285 were added later.

What are the 7 healing frequencies?

Usually 396, 417, 528, 639, 741, 852 and 963 Hz: the six original Solfeggio tones plus 963 Hz, paired with the seven chakras from root to crown.

Which chakra goes with which frequency?

In the common modern pairing: 396 Hz root, 417 Hz sacral, 528 Hz solar plexus, 639 Hz heart, 741 Hz throat, 852 Hz third eye and 963 Hz crown.

Where do Solfeggio frequencies come from?

They were made famous by Leonard Horowitz’s 1999 book Healing Codes for the Biological Apocalypse, which credits Joseph Puleo with finding them in the Book of Numbers. They are not medieval, although they borrow the names of medieval singing syllables.

Do Solfeggio frequencies work?

Calm music helps many people relax. For the specific numbers there is very little research: one small study (Akimoto et al., Health, 2018) found lower cortisol after 528 Hz music. There is no evidence that they heal illness.

Is 432 Hz a Solfeggio frequency?

No. 432 Hz is a proposed tuning for the note A, instead of the standard 440 Hz. It is a separate idea that is often mixed up with the Solfeggio list; our 432 Hz vs 528 Hz page compares the two.

Which Solfeggio frequency is the most popular?

Usually 528 Hz. Leonard Horowitz’s 1999 book singled it out as the “love frequency”, and that name has made it the best-known tone on the list.
