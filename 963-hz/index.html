The calling

When you sense there is something more

Some evenings the world feels too small. You have done everything you were supposed to do, and still a quiet part of you is looking up, as if listening for something just beyond hearing. 963 Hz is the tone many people turn to in those moments. It is the highest of the Solfeggio frequencies, a clear, bright note that seems to rise out of the body rather than settle into it.

The crown

The crown chakra and the violet light

In the chakra tradition, the crown sits at the very top of the head, where the self is said to open into everything beyond it. Its colour is violet, the last colour of the rainbow before light turns invisible. 963 Hz is the tone traditionally paired with it: a sound for remembering that you are part of something vast, and that the light you are looking for has always been inside you.

The name

Why it is called the frequency of the gods

Across meditation circles, 963 Hz carries names like “the God frequency” and “the frequency of oneness”. It joined the Solfeggio tones later than the first six, completing a scale of seven, one for each chakra, and it took the highest place: the crown. Many who listen describe a feeling of expansion, of the edges softening, of being held by something larger than themselves. However you name that something, the tone is an invitation to sit with it.

The ritual

A 963 Hz meditation for connection

Choose a quiet moment, ideally at the end of the day. Sit or lie down, let the light around you go dim, and play 963 Hz softly. Breathe in slowly and imagine violet light gathering at the crown of your head; breathe out and let it pour down through your whole body. Stay for ten minutes. If a word, an image or a feeling arrives, let it. Afterwards, sit for a breath before you return to the room.

Gentle care

Listening gently

A high tone is best kept soft. Play it low enough that you could speak over it, beneath music, singing bowls or rain if you like. Sound guidelines suggest keeping personal audio around 80 decibels or lower.

How to listen

What does 963 Hz sound like?

High and luminous, like the ring of a small bell held in the air, or light catching on glass. On its own it can feel bright, so most people let it shimmer quietly beneath slow piano, singing bowls or rain, and let it carry them inward.

Questions

What is 963 Hz?

A sound that vibrates 963 times a second, a little below the B two octaves above middle C. It is the highest of the nine Solfeggio frequencies and is usually paired with the crown chakra.

Why is 963 Hz called the God frequency?

It is a name used across meditation and frequency lists, because 963 Hz is the highest tone of the scale and is paired with the crown chakra, traditionally the chakra of the spiritual. The name has no single known origin.

Is 963 Hz one of the original Solfeggio frequencies?

No. The original six, made popular by Leonard Horowitz in 1999, are 396, 417, 528, 639, 741 and 852 Hz. 963 Hz was added in later lists to make seven tones, one per chakra.

What are the benefits of 963 Hz?

No study has tested 963 Hz on its own. People use it as a quiet, high tone for meditation, reflection and winding down, and calm sound with slow breathing helps many people relax. It is not a medical treatment.

Is 963 Hz dangerous?

No more than any other sound. Because it is high, keep the volume low; the WHO–ITU safe-listening standard suggests around 80 decibels or lower.

Which chakra and crystal go with 963 Hz?

Most lists pair 963 Hz with the crown chakra and violet stones such as amethyst. In Gemflow it belongs to amethyst.
