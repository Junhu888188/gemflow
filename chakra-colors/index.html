What is a chakra?

Chakra is Sanskrit for “wheel”. In yoga traditions, chakras are energy centres running up the spine. The rainbow colors came much later — a 20th-century Western addition. Older texts describe each centre with its own, quite different colours. We love the rainbow anyway, and the stones below are the ones most often paired with it.

Root · red · Ruby

Why was ruby called the king of gems?

In Sanskrit, ruby was called ratnaraj — “king of precious stones”.

The root chakra sits at the base of the spine. It stands for grounding: the floor everything else stands on. Red stones are its traditional match, and ruby is the oldest and most prized of them.

Source: G. F. Kunz, The Curious Lore of Precious Stones (1913)

Solar plexus · yellow · Citrine

Where does the name citrine come from?

Its name comes from the French citron — “lemon”.

For centuries citrine was sold under the name “topaz”. The solar plexus sits just above the navel and is linked with confidence, warmth and joy.

Heart · green · Emerald

Why did gem cutters stare at emeralds?

Pliny wrote that nothing is greener — gem cutters rested their tired eyes on it.

The heart chakra sits in the centre of the chest: love, and starting again. Green is the modern pairing; older sources describe the heart centre in other colours, which is a story of its own.

Source: Pliny the Elder, Natural History, Book 37

Throat · blue · Sapphire

What did “sapphire” first mean?

The ancient Greek sappheiros probably meant lapis lazuli — blue with flecks of gold.

The throat chakra is about voice: clarity, and saying what is true.

Crown · violet · Amethyst

Why is amethyst the “sober” stone?

Amethystos is Greek for “not drunk” — it was believed to keep you sober.

The crown chakra sits at the top of the head: calm, quiet, intuition.

Questions

What are the chakra colors?

In the modern Western system the seven chakras follow the rainbow: red (root), orange (sacral), yellow (solar plexus), green (heart), blue (throat), indigo (third eye) and violet (crown). This rainbow scheme is a 20th-century addition; older Indian texts give the centres different colours.

Which stone goes with each chakra color?

Common pairings are ruby for red, citrine for yellow, emerald for green, sapphire for blue and amethyst for violet.

What does chakra mean?

Chakra is Sanskrit for “wheel”. In yoga traditions, chakras are energy centres running up the spine.

Do chakra stones heal?

There is no scientific evidence that stones heal. People use them for meditation, ritual and as a reminder of an intention.
