Need a hand? Write to hello@gemflowcrystal.com. A real person reads every email, and we usually reply within two working days. It helps to mention your phone model and which stone you are using.

Restoring your stones

Stones you unlock are tied to your Apple ID or Google account, not to the app. On a new phone, or after reinstalling:

  • iPhone: open Gemflow and tap Restore purchases at the bottom of the main screen. Use the same Apple ID you bought with.
  • Android: sign in to Google Play with the same account. Your stones come back on their own when Gemflow opens.

Setting your stone as your Lock Screen (iPhone)

  • Tap Wallpaper on the main screen, then Save Live Photo. The first time, allow Gemflow to add photos to your library.
  • Open Settings › Wallpaper › Add New Wallpaper, tap Photos and choose your stone.
  • Keep the Live Photo button on, then tap Add.
  • The light moves for a moment each time you wake your phone. That short play is how iPhone handles Live Photo wallpapers.

Meditating with your stone

  • Guided: the light leads. Breathe in as it swells and out as it settles.
  • Follow my breath: hold anywhere on the screen to breathe in and release to breathe out. After a few breaths the light learns your pace and takes over.
  • My rhythm: once Gemflow has learned your pace, this replaces Guided, so next time you can simply begin. Choose Follow my breath again any time to relearn it.
  • The speaker button at the top right turns the music on or off. Your screen stays on during a session.

Common questions

Is there a subscription? No. Each stone is a one-time purchase, or you can unlock all of them at once, including stones we add later. There are no ads.

Do I need an account? No. Gemflow has no sign-in, and your settings stay on your phone.

How do I ask for a refund? Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. On iPhone, go to reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, open your order history in Google Play.

Is Gemflow a medical app? No. Gemflow is for relaxation and meditation. It does not diagnose, treat or cure any condition.

How do I delete my data? Everything the app saves lives on your phone, so deleting Gemflow removes it. See our privacy policy for details.
