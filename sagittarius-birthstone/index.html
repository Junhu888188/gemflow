What Is the Sagittarius Birthstone?

By Jun Hu

You're the girl who jokes about moving to Italy after one mildly disappointing afternoon, then secretly looks up apartments in Florence. Everyone thinks you're just having fun. Occasionally, you're being completely serious. Sagittarius (November 22 to December 21) has a birthstone called topaz, whose golden color mirrors your surprisingly tender inner world.

Dates
November 22 to December 21
Birthstone
Topaz
Color
Golden
In one line
Jokes about everything, dreams very seriously.

The Sagittarius everyone knows

Why Does Everyone Think Sagittarius Is So Carefree?

You're the friend who suggests going somewhere completely different when everyone has already agreed on a plan.

You make jokes at inappropriate moments, collect ridiculous stories, and somehow turn getting lost into the best part of an afternoon.

Your group chat has probably received a message from you beginning with "Okay, hear me out."

You're always imagining something more interesting than whatever happens to be on tomorrow's schedule.

Even the most sensible girl occasionally finds herself making wonderfully questionable plans with you.

The Sagittarius nobody sees

What Are You Really Searching For?

Behind all those jokes, you're surprisingly serious about finding a life that feels like yours.

You wonder whether you'll become the person you imagined when you were younger.

You secretly want someone who makes staying somewhere feel just as exciting as leaving.

In George Kunz's 1913 book The Curious Lore of Precious Stones, Sagittarius's stone is topaz.

Its golden color mirrors that quiet hope you carry everywhere, like late afternoon sunlight falling across an unfamiliar street.

There are some remarkably beautiful dreams living inside you.

Behind the endless jokes

What Is Sagittarius Hiding Behind Her Jokes?

You make a joke about something that genuinely hurt your feelings, and everyone assumes you've already moved on.

Excellent. That worked a little too well.

Sometimes being funny is easier than explaining why something upset you.

The Sagittarius symbol is an archer, always pointing toward somewhere beyond the present moment. You probably understand that feeling better than most.

Even when you're happy, a little part of you wonders what else might be possible.

People notice how easily you laugh. They rarely wonder what you've been quietly carrying around with you.

Somewhere beyond tomorrow

Where Do Your Dreams Take You at Night?

It's 2 a.m. You're looking at pictures of little European towns, imagining yourself buying flowers on a street where nobody knows your name.

Your playlist has become suspiciously sentimental.

You think about the people you might meet, the places you might fall in love with, and the girl you might eventually become.

Then you wonder whether the people you love now will still be part of that beautiful future.

Maybe golden is the color of all those possibilities.

Tonight, your favorite place to explore is the world inside you.

Topaz is the Sagittarius birthstone, a warm golden color that mirrors the endlessly hopeful dreamer inside you.
